# Changelog

## Unreleased (0.4.0)

### Added

- Monthly announcements ([#8](https://github.com/Trackberry/shiplog/issues/8)). A new entry kind, `announcement`, summarises a month: an intro, highlights linking to their permalinks and an "Also improved" list by area, in Markdown. `config.rewriter` drafts it from the month's approved features and changes and the small changes that got no entry (changes discarded as not customer-facing, merged pull requests without an entry, minus excluded labels and authors). Its subject is a new `Shiplog::Period` (one row per month), so drafting is idempotent. Without a rewriter the draft is a skeleton of the month's entries.
- `Shiplog::DraftAnnouncementJob` (schedule it monthly; it drafts the previous month and skips a month with nothing approved), `bin/rails shiplog:draft_announcement MONTH=2026-10`, and "Draft announcement for <month>" buttons in the inbox for the current and previous month.
- `config.announcements`: `:page` (default) renders `/changelog/2026-10`, links it from the month's heading ("Read the October 2026 summary") and adds it to the Atom feed (not to `?area=` feeds); `:external` publishes no page and links the month heading to `config.announcement_url = ->(announcement, locale) { url }`; `false` turns announcements off. Features and changes never get a `YYYY-MM` slug, so an announcement's URL is known when it is drafted (a pre-0.4.0 entry holding one makes the announcement use `YYYY-MM-update`).
- `config.on_announcement_approved = ->(announcement) { ... }` and the `announcement_approved.shiplog` notification, from `Shiplog::AnnounceJob`, once an announcement is approved and every configured locale has a current translation; once per approval (`shiplog_entries.announced_at`). Announcements offer `to_markdown(locale)`, `to_html(locale)`, `to_text(locale)` and `public_url`.
- The announcement's admin page: a rendered preview, Markdown, HTML and plain-text exports per locale, and whether your app was notified. The inbox flags an announcement whose month's entries changed since it was drafted.
- `Shiplog.unseen_announcements_count(since:)` and `shiplog_unseen_announcements_count(since)`, for a badge on the monthly summary rather than every entry. `config.on_changelog_seen` also runs on announcement pages, and public controllers expose `shiplog_entry` (the entry viewed).
- `Shiplog::Markdown.to_html` and `.to_text`: a small renderer that escapes everything it does not build (headings, paragraphs, lists, links and images with safe URLs only, emphasis, code, quotes, rules).
- New `config.classes` keys: `announcement`, `announcement_title`, `announcement_body`, `month_announcement`, `month_announcement_link`.
- Newsletter ([#8](https://github.com/Trackberry/shiplog/issues/8), off by default: `config.newsletter`). The approved announcement is emailed, in each recipient's language, to the host's users who opted in (`config.newsletter_recipients`, a callable; Shiplog stores nothing about them) and to the visitors who subscribed through Shiplog's form (`shiplog_newsletter_subscribers`, double opt-in with a 7-day signed link, the same answer whatever the address's state, a honeypot and `rate_limit` per address and IP). The form is on the changelog and announcement pages and `shiplog_newsletter_form` (`Shiplog::NewsletterHelper`) renders it anywhere.
- Unsubscribing: every newsletter carries the RFC 8058 one-click headers pointing at `POST /shiplog/newsletter/unsubscribe/<encrypted email>`, one endpoint for both lists, which records a suppression row in Shiplog (holding that one email, also for the host's users) and calls `config.on_newsletter_unsubscribe = ->(email) { ... }`; a suppressed address is skipped at send time whatever the host's list says, and `Shiplog::Newsletter.resubscribe(email)` lifts the suppression when a user opts in again. The footer links the same page (a button, nothing changes on a GET), `config.newsletter_unsubscribe_url` for the host's users, and a token-based preferences page for subscribers (language, unsubscribe, resubscribe, delete my data; the token is rotated after each change). The unsubscribe and preferences pages keep working while the newsletter is off. Tokens are encrypted (nothing readable in links or logs); `Shiplog::Newsletter::Tokens.rotate(old_secret_key_base)` keeps old links valid after a secret rotation. Confirmation links are single use. Log lines and the rate-limit cache carry digests of addresses, never addresses.
- Sending, from the announcement's admin page: "Send a test" to the admin's address in a chosen locale, and "Send…" with a confirmation step (recipients per locale) that enqueues `Shiplog::SendNewsletterJob`. Send is disabled, with the reasons shown, until the announcement is approved, every configured locale has a current translation, and `config.newsletter_from`, `config.newsletter_address` and `config.canonical_url` are set. One email per recipient, at most once (`shiplog_newsletter_deliveries`: recipient key, locale and outcome, no address, inserted before the send and purged after 180 days), merged and deduplicated by email (the host's recipient wins), read in batches of `config.newsletter_batch_size` (500) and claimed 100 at a time; sent, failed and pending counts, and "Retry failed" (an atomic reclaim; a reclaimed recipient that is gone ends as failed).
- `Shiplog::NewsletterMailer` (HTML in one 600px table column with inline styles and a text part, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, `config.newsletter_mailer_options` such as Postmark's `message_stream`, branding through `config.newsletter_logo_url` and `config.newsletter_accent`), its overridable templates under `shiplog/newsletter_mailer/`, and `Shiplog::NewsletterMailerPreview` (add `lib/shiplog/previews` to `action_mailer.preview_paths`). `Shiplog::Markdown.to_html` takes `styles:` for inline-styled tags.
- The admin's Newsletter page: subscriber counts per locale and status, a searchable list with remove, and a CSV export that neutralises formula-looking cells. `Shiplog::PurgeNewsletterJob` and `bin/rails shiplog:purge_newsletter` delete the pending subscribers older than 30 days.
- New `config.classes` keys: `newsletter`, `newsletter_title`, `newsletter_hint`, `newsletter_form`, `newsletter_label`, `newsletter_input`, `newsletter_button`, `newsletter_notice`. New I18n keys under `shiplog.newsletter` and `shiplog.admin.newsletter` (en and es).

### Changed

- `Shiplog::Drafter.draft` takes extra attributes for a new entry. The changelog's `_month` partial takes an optional `announcement:` local. Translations keep Markdown and link URLs.
- `Shiplog::Period.month_name(date, locale:)` names a month in any locale (English without month names); `Period#title` uses it. `Shiplog::PublicLinks.path` gives an engine route's path with the mount prefix.

### Upgrading

- Run `bin/rails shiplog:install:migrations` and `bin/rails db:migrate` (adds `shiplog_periods` and `shiplog_entries.announced_at`, then `shiplog_newsletter_subscribers` and `shiplog_newsletter_deliveries`). Rails reports no pending migration for an engine's migrations that were never copied.
- To send the newsletter: `config.newsletter = true`, `newsletter_from`, `newsletter_address` and `canonical_url`, plus `newsletter_recipients` and `on_newsletter_unsubscribe` for your own users, and `Shiplog::Newsletter.resubscribe(email)` where a user opts in again (see the README's Newsletter section). Give production a real `Rails.cache` (the subscribe form's rate limits count with it) and schedule `Shiplog::PurgeNewsletterJob` weekly. The newsletter's public pages live under `/shiplog/newsletter/`: a route constraint around the engine's mount must let `/shiplog/` paths through, as the webhook already needs. Shiplog stores one row per address that unsubscribes, your users' included: mention it in your privacy policy.
- With a custom `config.public_layout` or `config.classes`, style the `shiplog-newsletter*` classes (the subscribe form on the changelog and announcement pages, and the newsletter's pages). A host overriding `shiplog/changelogs/show` or `shiplog/changelog_entries/announcement` should render `shiplog/newsletter/form` to offer the form.
- A custom `config.prompt_builder` now also receives `kind: "announcement"`, with a different context (`month`, `entries`, `skipped`). Return `nil` for it to use the built-in prompt, or build an announcement prompt asking for a Markdown body.
- With `config.announcements = :page`, `/changelog/YYYY-MM` answers once that month's announcement is approved. A host route of that shape below the changelog path, declared after the mount, is shadowed for those months.
- Area detection never classifies announcements, and the changelog's "small changes" for an announcement leave out pull requests of a public issue's tree.
- Schedule `Shiplog::DraftAnnouncementJob` (see the README's Scheduling section), or set `config.announcements = false`.
- With a custom `config.public_layout` or `config.classes`, style the new classes. A host overriding `shiplog/changelogs/_month` should render the announcement link (see the engine's partial).

## 0.3.2 (2026-10-26)

0.3.1 hosts should upgrade.

### Fixed

- Boot failure in 0.3.1: when a host initializer or gem loaded `ActionView::Base` early (for example `ActionView::Base.field_error_proc = ...` in `config/initializers`), the engine's `action_view` hook ran before the autoloaders existed and raised `NameError: uninitialized constant Shiplog::HeadTagsHelper`. `Shiplog::HeadTagsHelper` now lives in `lib/shiplog/head_tags_helper.rb` and is required explicitly, so it is neither autoloaded nor reloaded (it is included into a class that never reloads).
- `shiplog_head_tags` raised `NoMethodError` in a view that lacks Shiplog's helpers, such as a ViewComponent or Phlex component rendered on a Shiplog page. It returns an empty string there: call it in the layout.
- `shiplog_head_tags` no longer autoloads `Shiplog::PublicController` (and the host's parent controller) when a host page or mailer renders a layout that calls it. Shiplog's public controllers answer a `shiplog_public_page?` marker instead.
- `og:image` and `Article.image` agree: `og:image` is the first screenshot whose URL can be built (it skipped to the callable when the first one had none), `Article.image` all of them, otherwise the `config.og_image` image.
- The `EXCEPT_KEYS` constant is no longer visible in the host's views (it moved to `Shiplog::HeadTags`).

### Changed

- The legacy `content_for :shiplog_og` block includes the `config.og_image` fallback image when it is set (it did so in 0.3.1; the 0.3.1 notes said it was exactly as in 0.3.0).

### Upgrading

- Bump `config.cache_version` when you change your layout's head or override a `shiplog/head/*` partial: ETags don't cover layouts.
- `config.og_image` output is not part of the ETag (only its presence is): bump `cache_version` when it changes.
- With `except: :canonical`, `og:url` still carries Shiplog's canonical (which honours `config.canonical_url`). A host writing its own canonical should make it agree, and should not emit one on search pages, where Shiplog sets `noindex` (`content_for(:shiplog_robots)` is `"noindex"` there).

## 0.3.1 (2026-10-26)

### Added

- schema.org structured data (JSON-LD) on the public pages, rendered by `shiplog_head_tags` (the legacy route is `content_for :shiplog_structured_data`; one `<script type="application/ld+json">` per page). A permalink page is an `Article` (`headline`, `description`, `inLanguage`, `datePublished`, `dateModified`, `url`, `mainEntityOfPage`, and when available `image`, `articleSection` and `publisher`, plus `isPartOf` the changelog); the changelog list is a `CollectionPage` with an `ItemList` of the entries on the page (left out for search results and an empty list); the roadmap is a `CollectionPage` with an `ItemList` of its cards. Titles and descriptions follow the request locale and `config.canonical_url`. The JSON is built from a Hash with `<`, `>` and `&` escaped as `\u003c`, `\u003e` and `\u0026`, so a `</script>` in a title cannot break out of the tag.
- `shiplog_head_tags(except: [])` (`Shiplog::HeadTagsHelper`, with the data side in `Shiplog::HeadHelper`): one call in a host layout's `<head>` renders the description, canonical link, robots, Open Graph, Twitter card, Atom link and JSON-LD of the public pages, through the overridable partials `shiplog/_head_tags` and `shiplog/head/_meta`, `_open_graph`, `_feed` and `_structured_data`. `except:` takes `:description`, `:canonical`, `:robots`, `:open_graph`, `:twitter`, `:feed` and `:structured_data`; other keys raise `ArgumentError`. It returns an empty string outside Shiplog pages. It is available in every view of the host (a Rails `action_view` load hook includes `Shiplog::HeadTagsHelper`, which holds only this method). **0.3.1 can fail to boot, see 0.3.2.** Shiplog's own layout uses it.
- A canonical link (new): a list page's own URL (its `area` and `page`, no other query parameters), a permalink's URL, honouring `config.canonical_url`. Search pages are `noindex` and have no canonical, so the two signals do not disagree. Twitter card tags (`twitter:card`, and `twitter:image`) are new too.
- `config.og_image` (`nil` or `->(entry, locale) { url }`, evaluated in the view): the share image of a permalink page without a screenshot, as `og:image` (with `og:image:alt`), `twitter:image`, `twitter:card` = `summary_large_image` and `Article.image`. Paths are made absolute; an error or a non-http(s) value is logged and ignored. Only its presence is part of `render_digest`, not its output: bump `config.cache_version` when the output changes.
- `config.structured_data` (default `true`; `false` turns the JSON-LD off). Part of `render_digest`, so changing it refreshes cached pages.

### Upgrading

- Custom `config.public_layout`: replace the `content_for(:shiplog_description)`, `:shiplog_og`, `:shiplog_feed`, `:shiplog_robots` and `:shiplog_structured_data` renders in the `<head>` with `<%= shiplog_head_tags %>`, and pass `except:` for the tags your layout writes itself (for example `except: [:open_graph, :twitter]` if it has its own share card). The `content_for` blocks still work (`:shiplog_og` now also carries the `config.og_image` fallback image), but are superseded: use `shiplog_head_tags` or the blocks, not both, because `shiplog_head_tags` does not read the blocks and rendering both duplicates the tags. Hosts that keep the blocks need no change.
- Hosts with a custom `config.public_layout` that stay on the blocks should render `<%= content_for(:shiplog_structured_data) %>` in the `<head>`, next to `content_for(:shiplog_og)`. Without it the pages work as before, with no structured data. Shiplog's own layout already does.

## 0.3.0 (2026-10-26)

### Added

- Entry permalinks: every approved feature and change has a page at `/changelog/:slug`, with a "Back to the changelog" link to its month, and links to the newer and older entries. The changelog list links each title to it. The slug is made from the title when the entry is first approved (`a-z`, `0-9` and dashes only, at most 80 characters, `-2`, `-3` for duplicates, `entry-<id>` when the title leaves nothing) and never changes afterwards. Drafts, discarded and roadmap entries and unknown slugs answer 404. The page sets `content_for :title`, `:shiplog_description` and `:shiplog_og` (Open Graph `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:type`, `og:url`); Shiplog's layout renders them, host layouts should too (see the README). The entry markup is the new `shiplog/changelogs/_entry_content` partial.
- An Atom feed of the latest 50 features and changes at `/changelog.atom`, with stable tag-URI ids, absolute URLs, an ETag, a `<link rel="alternate">` through `content_for :shiplog_feed` and a "Subscribe (Atom)" link on the changelog.
- `Shiplog.unseen_count(since:)` (approved features and changes approved after `since`, all of them when nil, at most 99), the `Shiplog::UnseenHelper#shiplog_unseen_count(since)` view helper, and `config.on_changelog_seen = ->(controller) { ... }`, called before each changelog index or permalink view renders (also when the answer is a 304), so a badge in the same response is current. It may write, so keep it cheap (the README shows a write guard). Shiplog doesn't know your users, so the host supplies and stores the time.
- `config.canonical_url` (nil by default): the origin (`"https://trackberry.com"`) used for the feed's ids, links and the other absolute URLs instead of the request's host. It and `config.product_name` are part of the pages' ETag.
- An unknown permalink slug answers 404 with `X-Cascade: pass`, so host routes declared after the engine mount below the changelog path (`/changelog/archive`) are no longer shadowed.
- `config.changelog_path` is validated leniently: surrounding slashes are stripped (`"/changelog"` works as before), segments may use letters, digits, `-` and `_` and may be nested (`"docs/whats_new"`). Blank values and anything unsafe (spaces, dots, `?`, `#`, accented letters) raise `ArgumentError`.
- `bin/rails shiplog:backfill_slugs`, which gives entries approved before 0.3.0 their permalink slug. The 0.3.0 migration already runs the same generation for every approved feature and change (oldest first), so the task is only needed for entries approved by older code during a deploy window. The public pages never write (a GET may hit a read replica): an approved entry without a slug is listed without a link and has no permalink page until the task runs.
- New `config.classes` keys: `permalink`, `permalink_back`, `permalink_nav`, `permalink_nav_link`. The Atom link reuses `footnote` and `footnote_link` (`shiplog-footnote__link--feed`).
- Product areas, detected by AI. The rewriter's reply may include `"area"`: an existing area (matched ignoring case, then by slug) or a new name of 1-3 words, created while fewer than `config.max_areas` (default 8) exist and the name is valid (at most 40 characters; letters, digits, spaces, `&`, `-`). The prompt lists the existing areas; a reply without `area`, or with a name that can't be used (cap reached, invalid), keeps the entry's current area. An area an admin chose on the entry edit page (`area_set_by_admin`) is never changed by a rewrite or by detection.
- An admin Areas page (in the nav): areas with their entry counts, rename (the slug follows), move up/down, merge (moves the entries, deletes the source) and delete (entries get no area), and a "Detect areas" button that enqueues `Shiplog::DetectAreasJob`. With no areas the job asks your `config.rewriter` to propose up to `max_areas` from up to 200 approved titles, then classifies the entries without an area in batches of 50 titles (at most 20 batches a run, idempotent, logged). The entry edit page gets an Area select (with "None") and the inbox shows an area badge.
- Area detection stamps every entry it asked the AI about (`area_detected_at`), so entries the AI declines are not asked again and later runs reach the rest; the Areas page has a "Re-check unplaced entries" checkbox to ask again. Only one detection runs at a time (a Postgres advisory lock).
- Area slugs for names that `parameterize` empties (Cyrillic, CJK) are the lowercased letters and digits joined by dashes (`Оплата` is `оплата`; `?area=` takes it URL-encoded). Area creation checks `config.max_areas` under a Postgres advisory lock, so concurrent rewrites cannot exceed it. Merging and deleting an area bump the entries' `updated_at`.
- The Areas page shows "Last detection: <time>, assigned N" or the last error. `Shiplog::DetectAreasJob` never retries or raises: failures are logged and recorded, and the Detect button refuses to enqueue without `config.rewriter`.
- On the public changelog, an area filter (`?area=slug`, an "All" link) when at least 2 areas have published entries; it combines with search and pagination and is part of the ETag. An unknown slug answers 404. Entries show their area as a tag (list and permalink), and the Atom feed adds `<category term="slug" label="Name"/>` and honours `?area=`.
- New `config.classes` keys: `areas`, `area_link` (with a `--current` modifier) and `entry_area`; new `config.max_areas`.
- Screenshots in entries (needs Active Storage). `Shiplog::Entry` gets `has_many_attached :images`, with the order in `image_order` and per-image alt texts in `image_alts` (both jsonb, keyed by blob id; the alt text defaults to the entry's title). When a draft is written (or regenerated), `Shiplog::RewriteEntryJob` fetches the issue's or pull request's `bodyHTML` (a separate small GraphQL query, `Queries::BODY_HTML`, so sync payloads don't grow) and downloads up to `config.max_images_per_entry` (4) images from GitHub's image hosts, only for a draft that has none: https only and exact host match (`private-user-images.githubusercontent.com`, `user-images.githubusercontent.com`, `camo.githubusercontent.com`, `raw.githubusercontent.com`, `objects.githubusercontent.com`, and `github.com/user-attachments/assets/<id>` with the id made of letters, digits and dashes only), at most 3 redirects (re-checked, their bodies read under the size cap), 5 second timeouts, 15 seconds per download and 45 per entry (monotonic clock; the remaining images are skipped and logged), 5 MB of raw bytes (`Accept-Encoding: identity`), PNG/JPEG/GIF/WebP by magic bytes (SVG and HTML are refused). The size is parsed from the header (PNG, GIF, WebP and JPEG): over 10,000 pixels on a side, over 40 megapixels or unreadable is refused, for admin uploads too, and the width and height go into the blob's metadata. The GitHub token is sent to `github.com` only. Failures are logged and skipped and never fail the draft.
- The admin entry edit page lists the images with alt text, Remove and Up/Down buttons, and an upload form (several files, checked by magic bytes and size on the server). The changelog list shows an entry's first image under its text, the permalink page all of them (`loading="lazy"`, alt text, width and height when Active Storage has analysed the image); the Atom feed and `og:image` use absolute URLs (`config.canonical_url` when set). The ETags include the blob ids, order and alt texts.
- `config.images` (default `true`; effective only with Active Storage) and `config.max_images_per_entry` (default 4), both validated and part of the pages' ETag. New `config.classes` keys: `entry_images` and `entry_image`. Without Active Storage (or with `config.images = false`) the feature is off, nothing crashes, and Shiplog logs once at boot when Active Storage is the reason.
- Translations of entries. `config.locales = [:es]` (default `[]`; an Array of Symbols or Strings, stored as symbols, the default locale dropped, part of the ETag) translates entries with your `config.rewriter` and publishes the translation with the English text, with no second approval. `Shiplog::TranslateEntryJob(entry, locale, force: false)` is enqueued per locale when an entry (roadmap entries too) is approved and when the English title or body of an approved entry changes. The prompt keeps meaning, tone, product names, numbers, links and formatting, puts the source inside `<source>` (neutralised) and asks for `{"title", "body"}`; the reply is validated and limited like the English kinds. Translations live in the new `shiplog_entry_translations` table (unique on `[entry_id, locale]`, `source_digest` of the English text, `edited_by_admin`). A translation made from the current English text is not made again (no LLM call), an admin's edit is kept until the English text changes (then replaced and logged), and the job writes nothing for an entry that is no longer approved. Failures are logged, retried twice with a growing wait, then dropped.
- Public pages show `Entry#display_title` and `#display_body` for a translated `I18n.locale`: the translation when it exists and is current, English otherwise, on the roadmap cards, the changelog list, the permalink page (title, body, meta description, Open Graph), the Atom feed and "Recently shipped". Translations are preloaded for the current locale in one query (`Entry.preload_translations`). Search matches current translations as well as English under a translated locale. Slugs stay English. The ETags include the translations shown; the feed's `xml:lang` follows the request locale.
- The entry edit page gets a Translations section per locale: title and body to edit (saving marks the translation as edited), a Missing, Stale, Up to date and Edited marker, and a Retranslate button (`force: true`).
- `config/locales/es.yml`: the gem's own public and admin texts in Spanish (and Spanish month names for its date formats), checked for completeness against `en.yml` by a test.

- `bin/rails shiplog:translate_approved`: enqueues `Shiplog::TranslateEntryJob` for every approved entry and configured locale that has no current translation, in batches (`BATCH_SIZE`, default 100), and prints the counts. Safe to repeat: current translations are not enqueued, and the job skips them anyway (digest).

- Translated area names. `shiplog_areas.name_translations` (jsonb, default `{}`, keyed by locale string; part of the 0.3.0 migration) holds the name in each of `config.locales`, and `Area#display_name(locale = I18n.locale)` returns the translation for a configured locale and the name otherwise. The public changelog uses it for the filter pills, the entry tags and the permalink tag, and the Atom feed for `<category label>`; slugs and the category `term` stay English, so `?area=` URLs are shared across locales. The pages' ETags include the translated names.
- `Shiplog::TranslateAreaJob(area, force: false)` makes one `config.rewriter` call for all locales: a short prompt (the name inside `<source>`, neutralised like the other prompts) asking for JSON `{"es": "..."}`. Every requested locale must come back as a valid area name (at most 40 characters; letters, digits, spaces, `&`, `-`), or the reply is rejected; failures are retried twice with a growing wait, then logged and dropped. It is enqueued when an area is created (by a rewrite, by detection) and when it is renamed, only with `config.locales` and `config.rewriter` set. Without `force` it fills the locales that have no name and never touches one that has (an admin's edit included), and makes no call when none is missing; a rename drops the stored translations and translates the new name.
- The admin Areas page shows a small field per configured locale on each area's form: saving without a rename keeps what the admin typed (an empty field removes that translation), and a rename discards translations sent with it. A "Translate names" button enqueues the job for the areas missing a translation in some locale; it only fills in the missing names, so edits are kept. `bin/rails shiplog:translate_approved` also enqueues the job (without force) for areas missing a translation, and prints how many.

### Changed

- The changelog's entry markup moved to `shiplog/changelogs/_entry_content.html.erb`; entry titles on the list are now links.

### Upgrade notes

- Copy and run the new migration: `bin/rails shiplog:install:migrations`, then `bin/rails db:migrate`. It adds `shiplog_entries.slug` (unique index on non-null values), an index on `shiplog_entries (status, approved_at)`, the `shiplog_areas` table and `shiplog_entries.area_id` (nullable foreign key, `ON DELETE SET NULL`, indexed) `shiplog_entries.area_set_by_admin` (boolean, default false) and `shiplog_entries.area_detected_at` (datetime), and the small key-value table `shiplog_settings` (the last area detection's result). The migration also gives the entries approved so far their slug. If old code approves entries during your deploy window, run `bin/rails shiplog:backfill_slugs` afterwards.
- `config.changelog_path` now raises `ArgumentError` for blank values and for characters outside `A-Z`, `a-z`, `0-9`, `-`, `_` and `/` (spaces, dots, `?`, `#`). Surrounding slashes are stripped, so `"/changelog"` and `"whats_new"` from a 0.2.0 initializer keep working.
- If you override `shiplog/changelogs/_entry.html.erb`, titles are not links in your copy; render `shiplog/changelogs/_entry_content` (locals `entry:`, `heading:`, `linked: true`) or link `changelog_entry_path(entry.slug)` yourself.
- If your `config.public_layout` is your own layout, render `content_for(:shiplog_og)`, `content_for(:shiplog_feed)` and the `:shiplog_description` meta tag in its `<head>` to get Open Graph tags and feed discovery on the permalink pages.
- New I18n keys, all with English defaults: `shiplog.permalink.title`, `.back`, `.nav_label`, `.newer`, `.older` (with `title`); `shiplog.feed.title_with_product` (with `product`), `.link_title`, `.subscribe`.
- Areas appear as you rewrite entries. To fill in existing ones, open the admin's Areas page and click "Detect areas" (it needs `config.rewriter`). Set `config.max_areas` to change the cap of 8 (`0` stops the AI creating areas).
- If you use a custom `config.prompt_builder`, ask your model for `"area"` in its JSON reply (an existing name, or a new one of 1-3 words), or entries get no area from rewrites. List the existing areas in your prompt with `Shiplog::Area.ordered.pluck(:name)`.
- If you override `shiplog/changelogs/_entry_content.html.erb` or `show.html.erb`, add the area tag (`entry.area`, class `entry_area`) and render `shiplog/changelogs/_areas` (locals `areas:`, `current:`, `query:`) to get the tag and the filter. The search partial reads the selected area from `@area` (its locals are still `query:` and `count:`, so a 0.2.0 copy keeps rendering, but its form and "Clear search" link don't carry the area: add `<input type="hidden" name="area" value="<%= @area.slug %>">` when `@area` is set, and `area: @area&.slug` to the clear link).
- New I18n keys, all with English defaults: `shiplog.changelog.areas.label` and `.all`; `shiplog.admin.areas.*`, `shiplog.admin.nav.areas`, `shiplog.admin.entries.no_area` and `shiplog.admin.entries.fields.area`.
- Every translation key of the public pages now has an English default (including `shiplog.roadmap.title` and `.lede`, `shiplog.changelog.title`, `.lede` and `.pagination.*`, `shiplog.columns.*`, `shiplog.empty`), so a host locale without Shiplog translations shows English instead of translation-missing spans.
- Screenshots need Active Storage: load it in the host (`require "active_storage/engine"` in `config/application.rb`, or `rails/all`) and install its tables (`bin/rails active_storage:install`). Without it everything else works and images stay off. The migration adds `shiplog_entries.image_order` (jsonb, default `[]`) and `shiplog_entries.image_alts` (jsonb, default `{}`) either way. The GitHub token needs no extra scope: the images come from signed URLs in the body's HTML (fetched with the token you already use for GraphQL) and from public GitHub image hosts. Entries drafted before the upgrade get images only by upload (or by "Regenerate" while they have none). Add `config.images = false` to opt out. Thumbnails in the admin are variants when `image_processing` is in your bundle and a processor is available (`ruby-vips` loads, or `mini_magick` is in the bundle); otherwise the original image is shown. Shiplog adds no dependency.
- If you override `shiplog/changelogs/_entry_content.html.erb`, render `shiplog_entry_images(entry, limit: 1)` (list) or `shiplog_entry_images(entry)` (permalink) under the body to show images. New I18n keys, all with English defaults: `shiplog.admin.entries.images.*`.
- Translations of entries approved before the upgrade: nothing translates them by itself (the job runs on approval and on English edits). After setting `config.locales` and `config.rewriter`, run `bin/rails shiplog:translate_approved` once; it enqueues one job per missing entry and locale, prints how many, and can be repeated (it only enqueues what has no current translation). Each job is one LLM call.
- Translated area names: the same migration adds `shiplog_areas.name_translations` (jsonb, not null, default `{}`). With `config.locales` and `config.rewriter` set, every new or renamed area costs one LLM call (areas are at most `config.max_areas`); run `bin/rails shiplog:translate_approved` (or click "Translate names" on the Areas page) once to translate the existing areas. New I18n keys, all with English defaults: `shiplog.admin.areas.translate_names`, `.translate_names_help`, `.no_locales`, `.no_rewriter_translate`, `.translation_queued`. If you override `shiplog/changelogs/_areas.html.erb`, `_entry_content.html.erb` or the feed template, use `area.display_name` instead of `area.name`.
- Translations: copy and run the new migration (see above): it also creates `shiplog_entry_translations` (`entry_id` foreign key with `ON DELETE CASCADE`, `locale`, `title`, `body`, `source_digest`, `edited_by_admin`, timestamps; unique index on `[entry_id, locale]`). Nothing changes until you set `config.locales`. Then every approval and every English edit of an approved entry costs one LLM call per locale, and entries approved before the upgrade have no translation until you run `bin/rails shiplog:translate_approved` (see above): they show in English meanwhile. Area names are translated with the entries (see above). Your routes decide which URLs set `I18n.locale` (a `/es` scope, a middleware); Shiplog adds none, and the locale must be in `I18n.available_locales` (the gem now ships `es`, so a host with `config.i18n.available_locales = [:en]` has to add it).
- Hosts with Spanish pages: the gem's `es.yml` now supplies `shiplog.*` and the month names (`date.month_names`); override any key in your own `es.yml`, or add `rails-i18n`, whose values for the months are the same. If you override `shiplog/roadmaps/_card.html.erb`, `_shipped.html.erb`, `shiplog/changelogs/_entry_content.html.erb`, `changelog_entries/show.html.erb` or the feed template, replace `entry.title` and `entry.body` with `entry.display_title` and `entry.display_body` to show translations. If you call `Entry.search(query)` yourself, pass `locale: I18n.locale` to search translations too. A custom `config.prompt_builder` does not apply to translations.
- Shipping `es.yml` adds `:es` to the derived `I18n.available_locales` for hosts that don't set it explicitly (Rails derives it from the loaded locale files), so a host that switches locale from the request now accepts `:es`. Hosts that set `config.locales = [:es]` and also set `I18n.available_locales` (or `config.i18n.available_locales`) explicitly must list `:es` in it.
- If you use `config.classes` with `config.stylesheet = false`, style the new elements, or they render unstyled (a full-size screenshot overflows the list): the permalink keys, `areas`, `area_link`, `entry_area`, `entry_images` and `entry_image`. The `--tailwind` install generator (`lib/generators/shiplog/install`) has Tailwind values for all of them to copy.
- If your layout shows the unseen count, it renders per-user content: declare `etag { current_user&.changelog_seen_at }` on your `public_parent_controller`.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-26)

### Added

- A Roadmap / Changelog switcher in the default page header. Add your own pages with `config.extra_pages` (`[{ label:, path: }]`, each a String or a callable evaluated in the view), and render the same switcher on host pages with `helper Shiplog::SwitcherHelper` and `shiplog_switcher`.
- Roadmap status by default: a rule and marker per column, a card tag with the issue's repository (`config.repository_labels`), "Includes N parts" on cards, a "Recently shipped" section (`config.roadmap_shipped_count`, default 4, `0` hides it) and an optional footnote with a "Suggest a feature" link (`shiplog.roadmap.footnote`, `config.feedback_path`).
- New `config.classes` keys: `switcher`, `switcher_link`, `column_marker`, `card_meta`, `card_tag`, `shipped`, `shipped_title`, `shipped_list`, `shipped_item`, `shipped_date`, `shipped_link`, `footnote`, `footnote_link`.
- Per-repository options in `config.repositories`: `{ name:, changelog: false, gem: }`. `changelog: false` keeps a repository on the roadmap only. `gem:` names the gem in your lockfile so that upgrading it produces a single changelog entry, written from the gem's own merged pull requests, instead of one entry per repository. `config.lockfile_path` changes the lockfile that is read.
- A Dependabot author, a `chore(deps)` title or the `dependencies` label no longer excludes a pull request that carries gem changes; other excluded labels, such as `internal`, still do.
- `config.cache_version`, to refresh cached public pages when the output of a configured callable changes.
- Search on the public changelog: a GET form (`/changelog?q=csv`) that lists approved features and changes whose title or body contain every word of the query, with a result count, a "Clear search" link and pagination that keeps `q`. Turn it off with `config.changelog_search = false`; override `shiplog/changelogs/_search.html.erb` to change it. New `config.classes` keys: `search`, `search_input`, `search_button`, `search_summary`, `search_clear`, `search_empty`. Search pages set `content_for :shiplog_robots, "noindex"` (emitted as a robots meta tag by Shiplog's own layout; add it to your layout, see the README).
- A Discard button on draft rows of the admin inbox, next to Edit and Approve. It updates the list in place; give a reason from the entry's page.

### Changed

- Roadmap drafts are shorter (about two sentences) and lead with what the customer gets, instead of opening with "We're working on".
- The roadmap's ETag also covers its recently shipped entries and each card's repository and sub-issues, so those changes reach cached pages.
- The migration `create_shiplog_pull_request_upgrades` also adds an index on `shiplog_entries (status, released_on)`.
- Gem upgrade detection: a failure on one pull request no longer blocks the others or keeps the scan window from advancing. Temporary GitHub errors are retried and given up after three attempts; other errors give up at once, with a warning. Pull requests merged before the next scan's start are marked checked without being fetched.
- Only the last commit's lockfile change is seen for a rebase merge; Dependabot's single-commit pull requests are unaffected.

### Fixed

- The scan window could move back to the oldest pending lockfile pull request after a first sync of a long history, drafting every old pull request and commit in that span. It now never ends before its start.
- Hosts that mount the engine with another name (`as: :updates`) no longer get a `NameError` from the switcher.
- The public pages' ETag used a per-process `Hash#hash`, so workers of a multi-process host answered with different ETags for the same page. It is now a stable digest.

### Upgrade notes

- Copy and run the new migration: `bin/rails shiplog:install:migrations`, then `bin/rails db:migrate`. Run it before the job workers start on the new code, because the importer writes the new columns.
- New I18n keys, all with English defaults, so a locale without translations shows English: `shiplog.switcher.label`, `.roadmap`, `.changelog`; `shiplog.roadmap.parts` (with `count`), `.shipped_heading`, `.shipped_date_format`, `.full_changelog`, `.suggest`; `shiplog.changelog.search.label`, `.submit`, `.summary` (with `count` and `query`), `.clear`, `.empty` (with `query`).
- The default `shiplog/_page_header` now renders the switcher. Hosts that override `_page_header` keep theirs; render `shiplog_switcher` (or the `shiplog/switcher` partial) in it to get the links.
- If your app overrides `roadmaps/show`, `_column` or `_card` only to add column status, card tags or recently shipped, delete them and use the new defaults and `config.classes` keys.
- Style the new elements with the new class keys above if you use `config.classes` with `config.stylesheet = false`.
- `extra_pages` paths must be absolute (`/…`), `http(s)://` or `mailto:`. A `feedback_path` that is not one of these renders no link.
- Repository names are validated: anything other than `owner/name` raises `ArgumentError` when `config.repositories` is assigned.
- For gem upgrades the GitHub token needs read access to **Contents** and **Pull requests** on the gem repositories. Only pull requests synced by 0.2.0 or later are inspected; earlier merges are not backfilled.

## 0.1.1 (2026-10-26)

### Added

- Admins can switch a changelog entry between New feature and Improvement.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-26)

Initial release.

- Public roadmap and changelog engine (`Shiplog::Engine`) and a separate admin engine (`Shiplog::Admin::Engine`) with a drafts inbox, entry editing, a roadmap board with drag and drop, and sync status.
- GitHub GraphQL sync across several repositories, a signed webhook endpoint (`issues`, `pull_request`, `sub_issues`), and scheduled jobs (`Shiplog::SyncJob`, `Shiplog::ScanChangesJob`).

- Styling: `config.classes` (per-element classes next to stable `shiplog-*` BEM classes), `config.stylesheet`, and a bundled stylesheet in `@layer shiplog` with `--shiplog-*` variables. Public views are split into overridable partials.
- Rendering inside host layouts: `public_parent_controller` / `public_layout`, `admin_parent_controller` / `admin_layout` / `admin_chrome` / `admin_flash`, and unknown `*_path` / `*_url` helpers forwarded to `main_app`.
- Admin access fails closed (`config.authorize_admin` defaults to deny).
- `rails generate shiplog:install` (with `--tailwind`) and a `shiplog-feature.yml` issue form.
